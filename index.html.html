Our method

How we assess every opportunity

Before anything reaches the network, we underwrite it across four dimensions — a disciplined, repeatable framework that surfaces the genuine investment case, and flags anything that doesn't stand up.

01

Purchase price

Priced on value, or on hope?

Benchmarked against comparable sales and genuine market value — never simply the asking price.

02

Estimated rent

What will it realistically let for?

Achievable rent modelled from live local letting data and demand — grounded figures, not optimism.

03

Gross & net yield

Does the income case stand alone?

We work the return through before any assumptions about future growth are made.

04

Potential upside

Where is value to be added?

We identify the levers — refurbishment, re-letting, planning, repositioning — that grow rent and capital value.
