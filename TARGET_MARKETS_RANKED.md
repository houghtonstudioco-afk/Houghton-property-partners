# The 8 best markets, re-ranked for LinkedIn, phone and walk-ins

Dropping the email-only constraint changes the order significantly. Email
reachability was the third filter in every earlier ranking, and it was
penalising sectors that are easy to reach in person or on LinkedIn but publish
no addresses. Prestige car dealerships went from "avoid" to top of the list on
that change alone.

Scored 1-10 on the three things asked for. **Access** now means "can I get to
someone who can say yes, by any channel" rather than "is there an inbox".

## 1. Prestige and independent car dealers

**Why it's now first.** The need data is the strongest of any sector examined:
**56% of leads arrive after business hours**, **43.2% of sales leads are
mishandled**, and **14.1% never reach the CRM at all**. **76% of dealership
executives are increasing AI budgets** in 2026, and **55% of adopters report a
10-30% profit lift** inside a year.

**Access, now that walking in counts:** a prestige showroom mid-week is quiet
and the sales manager or dealer principal is on the floor. That is a
conversation almost nobody else is having with them, and it plays to a local
operator over a national vendor.

**Money:** gross profit per prestige unit is £10-20k. Do not say a lost £200k
Porsche enquiry is £200k lost — margins are 12-15% of proceeds and a dealer
principal knows that better than anyone.

**Watch out:** Impel and similar are selling AI lead-response into automotive
hard. Target independents and small groups, never Sytner-scale franchises.

## 2. Recruitment agencies

**Why it jumped.** Access flipped from moderate to best-in-class. Recruiters are
the most LinkedIn-native profession that exists — they live on the platform all
day. UK recruitment agencies show **10-18% reply rates and 25-35% connection
acceptance**, and the data explicitly notes these leads "don't get bombarded by
automated tools." HR and talent roles have the **highest reply rate of any
function at 12.08%**, against 6.98% for C-level.

**Money:** placement fees are five figures, so one extra placement pays for a
year of retainer.

**Need:** CV screening is the cleanest demo you have. You can run it against a
live vacancy on a first call and they see the shortlist.

## 3. Private dentistry, implant and cosmetic led

**The best all-rounder.** Implant and Invisalign cases are £3-15k, so one
recovered enquiry pays for months. The principal dentist usually owns the
practice, and you can **physically walk in** — reception is right there, and
asking for the practice manager is completely normal.

Filter hard for implant, All-on-4, Invisalign and cosmetic work. A
predominantly-NHS practice has neither the case values nor the budget.

## 4. Aesthetics and cosmetic clinics

**Access is the appeal.** The owner is the clinician, so there is exactly one
person to convince and they are on site. Walk in between appointments.

Slightly lower ceiling than dentistry on deal size, and you must filter out
single-operator salons — a Hotmail address and a mobile means no retainer
budget.

## 5. Insurance brokers

Independent brokers carry a **29-39 hour weekly admin burden, 8-10 hours of it
on email**. Revenue is commission and renewals, so they already think in
recurring terms and understand what an hour reclaimed is worth. FCA-regulated,
so compliance admin is heavy and non-negotiable.

Phone is the channel — office-based, and a receptionist will put you through to
a named director.

## 6. Multi-office accountancy

Partner decides, and the pain has a date on it: **records chasing in December
and January**. Multi-office firms multiply it. Approach now, because from
mid-November they will not take the call.

## 7. Private hospitals and cosmetic surgery groups

**Highest value per deal, worst access of the eight.** Self-pay surgery runs
into five figures, so the ROI case is trivial to make. But you cannot walk into
a private hospital and ask for the decision maker, and there are management
layers between reception and budget.

LinkedIn to a named operations or marketing lead is the only viable route.
Worth two or three carefully-researched approaches, not a campaign.

## 8. Estate and letting agents

**Included for one reason: access is perfect.** High street premises, walk in
any day, the branch manager is behind a desk. Money is the weakest of the eight
— thinner margins than clinics — but if you want reps and live conversations
this week, this is where you get them fastest.

You have already emailed 19 of these, which makes a walk-in warm rather than
cold: "I emailed you last week, I'm local, thought I'd introduce myself."

## What I would actually do this week

**Monday to Wednesday, walk in:** dental practices and aesthetics clinics near
you, plus any prestige dealer within driving distance. Aim for 5-6 conversations
a day. Nobody else is doing this and it converts far better than email.

**Every morning, LinkedIn:** 10 personalised connection requests to recruitment
agency directors. Personalised requests get **42% acceptance against 12% for
generic**, and referencing something they actually posted lifts reply rates
**89%**. That is the whole game — never send a template.

**Tuesday to Thursday mid-morning, phone:** insurance brokers and accountancy
partners.

**Park the private hospitals** until you have two or three case studies. Their
ROI case is the best of the lot, which is exactly why they will want proof.
